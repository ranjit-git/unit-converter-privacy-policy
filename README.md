# Privacy Policy for Unit Converter App

**Effective date:** June 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unit Converter App (the "App") handles information when you use the App on Android or iOS.

## Overview

Unit Converter App is a utility application that helps users convert units such as length, weight, speed, temperature, volume, currency, storage, time, epoch, angle, and pressure. The App is designed to work primarily on-device and does not require account creation to use its core features.

## Information Collected

The App is designed to minimize data collection.

### Information stored on your device

The App may store limited data locally on your device to support app functionality and improve your experience, including:

- Your selected app language.
- Your favorite converters.
- Your recently used converters.
- Cached currency exchange rate data used to speed up currency conversion results.
- App settings such as haptics preferences, if enabled in the app.

This information is stored locally using device storage and is not intended to identify you personally.

### Information processed temporarily

When you use the App, values that you type for conversions are processed temporarily in the app to calculate results. These values are not used to identify you and are not sold.

For the currency converter feature, the App may request live exchange-rate data from a third-party currency data source. This request may include the base currency needed to return conversion rates.

## Personal Data

The App does **not** require you to create an account.

The App does **not** intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, payment information, government identifiers, health information, messages, photos, or audio recordings.

The App does **not** sell personal information.

## Internet Access

The App may use an internet connection for limited purposes, including:

- Fetching live currency exchange rates.
- Opening external links such as the privacy policy page or app store listing.
- Sharing app links if you choose to use the share feature provided by your device.

Except as described above, core unit conversions are intended to work locally in the app.

## Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party packages or services for specific functionality, such as:

- Currency exchange-rate retrieval.
- Sharing content through the device's native share sheet.
- Opening external links in a browser or app store.

These third-party services may process technical information needed to perform their function, such as network requests, device or app metadata, or IP address information handled as part of standard internet communication. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## Analytics and Advertising

At this time, the App does **not** include third-party advertising SDKs.

At this time, the App does **not** include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs based on the currently implemented features reviewed for this policy.

If analytics, advertising, sign-in, cloud sync, crash reporting, or similar features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those features are released, as required.

## Children

The App is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed specifically to children under 13.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If it is discovered that personal information from a child has been collected inadvertently, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it.

## Data Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you clear the app's data, uninstall the App, or remove the relevant settings through device or app controls where available.

Cached currency data may be refreshed or overwritten automatically during normal app use.

## Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to reduce unnecessary data handling by keeping most functionality on-device and limiting stored information to what is needed for app features.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Your Choices

Depending on your device and app version, you may be able to:

- Change your language preference in the App.
- Manage your favorites and recent converters within the App.
- Disable haptics in the App, if that setting is available.
- Stop local data storage by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.
- Avoid live currency lookups by not using the currency converter feature while offline or by choosing not to refresh rates.

## Google Play Data Safety Guidance

Based on the current implementation reviewed for this policy, the App is intended to use local storage for app functionality and may access the network only for currency rates and opening user-requested external links.

Google Play Console disclosures must always match the actual shipped app build. If app behavior changes, the Data safety form and this Privacy Policy should be updated accordingly before publication.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app changes, legal requirements, or operational updates. The updated version will be posted at the privacy policy URL provided for the App, and the effective date will be revised.
